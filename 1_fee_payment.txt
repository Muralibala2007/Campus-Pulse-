Subject: Payment of Tuition and Development Fees for Academic Year 2026-27

All students of SE and TE (Computer, IT, EXTC) are hereby informed that the
second installment of tuition fees is to be paid through the college ERP portal
on or before 10 October 2026. Payment by cash at the counter is discontinued.
Students availing the government scholarship should upload the sanction letter
on the portal. Fee receipts must be submitted to the class coordinator.

Students are advised to keep a printed copy of the receipt for future reference.
